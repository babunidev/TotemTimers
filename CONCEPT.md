# TotemTimers — concept

**Author:** Babuni.dev

A compact, draggable Shaman status interface for WoW 1.12 that reports elemental totem duration, shields and weapon imbues.

## Source-observed constraints

The interface stays close to Blizzard's visual language and only appears for Shamans; totem state, shield charges and weapon effects are grouped in one tool.

This is not an invented account of early discussion.
